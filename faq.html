Before you order

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear expectations help us build the right solution for your business. Please review these answers before placing an order.

1. Who pays for hosting and payment processing?

You create and own your hosting and payment-provider accounts. Hosting subscriptions, transaction fees, payment processing charges and other third-party service costs are your responsibility unless we explicitly agree otherwise in writing. We can help configure or connect these services when included in your project scope.

2. Which payment providers can I use?

Provider availability depends on your country, business location and eligibility. Please confirm which providers are available to you before ordering. We will agree on a suitable integration approach before development begins.

3. Do I need to supply my product information?

Yes. For e-commerce projects, you supply your product files, accurate descriptions, prices, images and other required business information. Copywriting, photography, bulk product uploads and content creation are included only when explicitly listed in your package or custom offer.

4. What if I need additional features?

Each package covers only the features and deliverables listed in its description. Additional pages, integrations, automations, advanced functionality or other work outside the agreed scope require a separate custom offer and your approval before work begins.

5. Who owns my website and accounts?

You retain ownership and control of the business accounts you create, including your domain, hosting and payment-provider accounts. Ownership and delivery of project-specific source code, designs and other deliverables follow the terms agreed for your project. Third-party software, plugins and services remain subject to their own licences and terms.
